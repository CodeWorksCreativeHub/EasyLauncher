<b>Features:</b>

- Favorite App Quick Access: Display your most-used apps on the home screen in a clean text format for fast access.
- Hide Apps: Keep your home screen minimal by hiding selected apps.
- Rename Apps: Customize app names, synced across favorites and app lists.
- App Locking: Protect selected apps and settings with biometric authentication.
- Privacy Focused: No INTERNET permission used, ensuring your data stays local.
- Crash Reports: Easily report issues via email with logs for debugging.

<b>Customization:</b>

- Font Colors: Choose custom colors with a built-in picker.
- Alignment: Position date, time, and apps (left, center, right).
- Padding: Adjust spacing between elements.
- Font Size: Set individual sizes for each home screen element.
- Toggle Visibility: Show or hide elements as needed.

<b>Gestures:</b>

- Swipes: Navigate using up, down, left, and right gestures.
- Double Tap: Trigger custom actions instantly.
- Clock Tap: View alarms or world clock.
- Date Tap: Open calendar.
- Battery Tap: View battery usage.

<b>Actions:</b>

- Launch Apps: Open any selected app quickly.
- App List: View and manage all installed apps.
- Screen Lock: Lock your device instantly.
- Recent Apps: Access recently used apps.
- Notifications: Open notification drawer.
- Quick Settings: Access system controls quickly.
- Power Menu: Restart, power off, and more.
- Screenshot: Capture your screen instantly.
- Disable Gestures: Turn off gestures when needed.

<b>Permissions:</b>

- SET_WALLPAPER: Change device wallpaper.
- EXPAND_STATUS_BAR: Access notifications and quick settings.
- USE_BIOMETRIC: Enable fingerprint/biometric security.
- VIBRATE: Provide haptic feedback.
- QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Display installed apps.
- REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: Request app uninstall (restricted).
- SET_ALARM: Schedule alarms.
